## Overview

Tube PIP is a lightweight YouTube client that brings core YouTube Premium features to iOS completely free. Designed for focused viewing, it enables full background playback and floating window Picture-in-Picture without requiring expensive subscriptions or sideloading `.ipa` files.

## Key Features

* **Native Background Playback:** Listen to videos, podcasts, or music with your iPhone screen turned off or while using other applications.
* **Picture-in-Picture (PIP):** Watch videos in a resizable, draggable floating window overlay across iOS.
* **Distraction-Free Interface:** Hides addictive short-form algorithm feeds to prioritize long-form media.
* **Ad Suppression Support:** Pairs seamlessly with encrypted DNS solutions (such as AdGuard DNS via DNSecure) for ad-free playback.
