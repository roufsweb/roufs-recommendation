## Overview

TubeMax is a high-performance video player engineered for power users who want advanced control over streaming media. It unlocks background playback, floating Picture-in-Picture, multi-track audio selection, and manual resolution overrides on iOS.

## Key Features

* **Multi-Track Audio Switching:** Effortlessly switch between different language audio streams and commentary channels on supported videos.
* **Manual Resolution Forcing:** Lock video streams to high resolutions (including 1080p and 4K) without automatic mobile throttling.
* **Background Audio & PIP:** Smooth playback continuation with screen locked or while operating other iOS apps.
* **Built-in Ad Suppression:** Clean viewing experience with automatic avoidance of disruptive mid-roll ads.
