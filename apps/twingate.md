## Overview

Twingate is the single best modern solution for connecting securely to your home network, NAS, media server, or homelab from anywhere in the world. It completely replaces legacy, vulnerable VPN setups (like OpenVPN port forwarding) with enterprise-grade Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA). You never have to expose your home router's public IP address or open firewall ports.

## Key Features

* **Zero Open Ports Required:** The lightweight Twingate Connector runs inside your home network (via Docker, Raspberry Pi, or Linux VM) and initiates outbound-only secure tunnels.
* **Invisible Home Network:** Because no open ports exist on your home router, your home network is completely invisible to malicious internet port scanners.
* **Resource-Level Granular Access:** Grant access strictly to specific IP addresses and internal port ranges (e.g., only your Jellyfin server on port 8096) rather than exposing the whole subnet.
* **Seamless Split-Tunneling:** Only traffic designated for your home lab routes through the tunnel; all standard web surfing continues over your device's normal high-speed connection without battery drain.
* **Native Single Sign-On (SSO):** Authenticate securely via Google, GitHub, or Microsoft with hardware MFA/passkey support.
