## Overview

DNSecure allows you to easily manage and switch your device's DNS resolver profiles. It is a lightweight, privacy-conscious utility that leverages native iOS encrypted DNS capabilities (NetworkExtension) without battery drain or continuous background process overhead.

## Key Features

* **System-Wide Ad & Tracker Suppression:** By pairing with an ad-filtering DNS provider (such as AdGuard DNS), you can block ads across mobile browsers and apps without running a full battery-heavy VPN.
* **Modern Encrypted Protocols:** Full native support for DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) and DNS-over-TLS (DoT) to safeguard DNS queries from ISP inspection.
* **Low System Overhead:** Operates at the OS networking layer with zero background battery consumption.

## Recommended AdGuard Setup

1. Open DNSecure and tap to add a DNS-over-HTTPS provider.
2. Enter the DoH URL: `https://dns.adguard-dns.com/dns-query`
3. Save and enable the profile in iOS Settings -> General -> VPN & Device Management -> DNS.
