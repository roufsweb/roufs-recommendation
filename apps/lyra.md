## Overview

Lyra is a top-tier free alternative to paid music streaming platforms like Spotify Premium and Apple Music. It brings a sleek, intuitive player interface and access to vast catalogs of audio tracks, allowing you to curate playlists and stream music on iOS without recurring fees.

## Key Features

* **Vast Music Catalog:** Search and stream high-quality music tracks and audio broadcasts.
* **Background Audio Playback:** Listen uninterrupted with your iPhone screen locked or while running other apps.
* **Custom Playlists:** Build, organize, and sort unlimited personal music playlists.
* **Clean Player Interface:** Modern, distraction-free playback controls with full lock-screen media controls.
