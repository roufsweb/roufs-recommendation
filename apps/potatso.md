## Overview

Potatso is widely regarded as the best free, manual-configuration proxy client for iOS. Unlike standard commercial VPN apps that lock users into specific paid servers, Potatso gives you granular, protocol-level control over your network routing. It operates entirely locally on-device without telemetry or forced subscriptions.

## Key Features

* **Universal Protocol Support:** Native support for Shadowsocks, SOCKS5, HTTP, and HTTPS proxies.
* **Manual & Granular Configuration:** Add proxy nodes manually by IP, port, encryption algorithm, and password, or scan QR codes.
* **Smart Rule Routing:** Route traffic selectively based on domains, IP ranges, or geo-locations (direct connection for local sites, proxy for foreign traffic).
* **Network Logging & DNS Customization:** Inspect local network requests and enforce custom upstream DNS servers.
* **Battery & Memory Efficient:** Lightweight background packet tunnel using native iOS NetworkExtension framework.
