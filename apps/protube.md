## Overview

ProTube is a dedicated video player focused on delivering a clean, ad-free video experience on iOS. Designed for users who want premium streaming capabilities without commercial interruptions, it brings essential desktop-grade video conveniences to mobile.

## Key Features

* **Built-in Ad Suppression:** Automatically skips and blocks intrusive video pre-rolls and mid-roll commercials.
* **Picture-in-Picture (PIP):** Float video playback anywhere across your iOS home screen while navigating other applications.
* **Background Audio Mode:** Lock your device or switch apps while video audio continues streaming uninterrupted.
* **Minimalist Interface:** Reduces feed clutter, hides algorithmic distractions, and focuses purely on high-resolution video playback.
