## Overview

LocalSend is the ultimate open-source alternative to Apple's AirDrop. It allows you to share files, photos, videos, directories, and text between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network without relying on the internet, third-party cloud servers, or platform lock-in. It bridges the gap between Apple, Android, Windows, and Linux hardware effortlessly.

## Key Features

* **True Cross-Platform Sharing:** Transfer data seamlessly between an iPhone, iPad, Android phone, Windows PC, Mac, and Linux laptop.
* **100% Offline & Private:** Communication takes place directly over your local area network (LAN) using end-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption. Files never touch external cloud servers.
* **No Account Required:** Zero registration, phone numbers, or email sign-ups needed. Devices discover each other automatically on the local network.
* **Uncapped Transfer Speeds:** Data transfer rate is limited only by your local Wi-Fi router speed, easily reaching hundreds of megabytes per second.
* **Batch Transfers & Directory Trees:** Send full folders and bulk image galleries without compression or metadata stripping.
