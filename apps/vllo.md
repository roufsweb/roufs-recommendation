## Overview

VLLO is an intuitive, multi-track mobile video editing suite tailored for creators, vloggers, and social media video production. Unlike competing mobile video editors that slap intrusive branding watermarks across exported videos unless you pay, VLLO allows clean, watermark-free high-resolution video exports on its free tier.

## Key Features

* **Watermark-Free Exports:** Export standard high-definition videos with zero forced branding logos or watermarks.
* **Multi-Track Timeline:** Layer background video, B-roll, overlay audio, sound effects, voiceovers, and animated subtitles on individual timeline tracks.
* **Precision Touch Trimming:** Intuitive timeline gestures make splitting, ripple-deleting, and reordering video clips fast and responsive on touchscreens.
* **Built-in Royalty-Free Asset Library:** Includes access to copyright-cleared background music tracks, sound effects, and transitions.
