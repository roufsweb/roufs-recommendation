## Overview

Aloha Browser offers the single best ad-free video and movie watching experience on iOS. While marketed as a privacy-focused browser, its standout capability is its custom, built-in native video player which is near-perfect for streaming web videos, movies, and episodic series without intrusive popups or disruptive ad scripts.

## Key Features

* **Near-Perfect Native Video Player:** Replaces clunky web video players with a smooth, native iOS player featuring intuitive swipe controls for brightness, volume, and scrubbing.
* **Background Audio & PIP:** Continue listening to movies or documentaries when the screen is locked or while multitasking in other apps via Picture-in-Picture.
* **Aggressive Ad & Popup Blocking:** Blocks video pre-rolls, mid-roll video ads, tracking scripts, and aggressive redirection popups out of the box.
* **Integrated Media Downloader:** Download videos directly to your device for offline viewing without requiring third-party shortcut tools.
* **Theatre & VR Mode:** Watch videos in a distraction-free cinematic player or connect VR headsets for a virtual cinema environment.
